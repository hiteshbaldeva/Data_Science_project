Executive Summary: Successfully engineered, cross-validated across 5 folds, and externally validated a Hierarchical Multimodal Cross-Attention Network achieving 85.0% Recall and 85.2% Accuracy (AUROC 0.894) on the 500-subject cohort, and 100% Sensitivity with 0.990 AUROC on the 120-subject external community cohort.

1. Dataset & Class Imbalance

  • Target Condition: Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction (LVEF ≤ 40%).
  • In-House Cohort: 500 subjects, 2,000 recordings (APEX, LLSB, LUSB, RUSB).
  • Prevalence: 60 positive (12.0%) vs. 440 negative (88.0%) — 7.33:1 imbalance ratio.
  • Mitigation: Square-root class weighting (~2.7x), prevalence-matched thresholding (τ = 0.20), cardiological site weighting, and strict subject-level 5-fold stratification.
